How status is read

Every captive-plan location is colored by its balance vs plan: short (on hand below requirement), even (on hand equals requirement), or surplus (on hand above requirement). Dot size scales with the size of the gap. A shared-pool diamond marks a station whose work is covered by a pool shared with another terminal and counted there (it shares power across stations). A free low/mid HP dot marks a location holding Available LHP/MHP units (under 4000 hp) not assigned to any captive job — deployable power to cover shortages, failures, or inspection swaps.

Inspection due (overlay) — an amber ring on locations holding available units with a forecast inspection within 3 days or overdue; overdue rings pulse. Swap before it lapses.

Requirement is the shared-job-adjusted unit count (shared jobs counted once), including active M schedules; excluding HHP, CALF, and slug units. On hand counts Available units under 4000 hp, excluding YBU/HBU boosters (slugs); a unit out working its captive train counts at its home terminal (in service there), not where it momentarily sits. HHP (≥4000 hp) is excluded from both shortage and surplus. Each location lists its captive trains/jobs and the units currently assigned to them (↗ = out on the road). Jobs whose pool is shared across terminals are counted once at a primary station and flagged on both sides.

Dead unit — set out dead in transit LD / DIT / DM — low/mid HP only (HHP excluded from routing). Move to its assigned shop; flagged if none assigned.

Lines march from each dead unit toward its assigned shop. Click any marker or action for detail.
